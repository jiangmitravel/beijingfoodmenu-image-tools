# Privacy Policy for Beijing Food Menu Image Processor

**Last Updated:** January 10, 2026

## Overview
Beijing Food Menu Image Processor ("the Extension") is designed with a privacy-first approach. We believe your photos and data belong to you.

## Data Processing
-   **Local Processing:** All image processing (compression, resizing, metadata removal, format conversion) happens entirely within your browser on your local device.
-   **No Uploads:** Your images are **never** uploaded to our servers or any third-party servers.
-   **No Tracking:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal usage data, cookies, or analytics.

## Permissions Usage
-   **activeTab / <all_urls> / scripting:** Used solely to access images on the web pages you choose to process (via the "Process All Images" context menu).
-   **downloads:** Used to save your processed images (single files or ZIP archives) to your computer.
-   **contextMenus:** Used to add the "Process Image" options to your browser's right-click menu.

## Third-Party Libraries
The extension uses the open-source library `JSZip` for creating ZIP archives locally. This library runs within the extension sandbox and does not transmit data.

## Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via our [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/jiangmitravel/beijingfoodmenu-image-tools).
