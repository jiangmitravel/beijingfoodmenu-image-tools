# Privacy Policy for {{EXTENSION_NAME}}

**Last Updated:** January 12, 2026

## Overview
{{EXTENSION_NAME}} ("the Extension") is designed with a privacy-first approach. We believe your photos and data belong to you.

## Data Processing
-   **Local Processing:** All image processing (compression, resizing, metadata removal, format conversion) happens entirely within your {{BROWSER_NAME}} on your local device.
-   **No Uploads:** Your images are **never** uploaded to our servers or any third-party servers.
-   **No Tracking:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal usage data, cookies, or analytics.

## Permissions Usage
-   **activeTab / <all_urls> / scripting:** Used solely to access images on the web pages you choose to process (via the \"Process All Images\" context menu).
-   **downloads:** Used to save your processed images (single files or ZIP archives) to your computer.
-   **contextMenus:** Used to add the \"Process Image\" options to your {{BROWSER_NAME}}'s right-click menu.
-   **storage:** Used to temporarily store your image queue and settings locally in your {{BROWSER_NAME}}.

## Third-Party Libraries
The extension uses the open-source library `JSZip` for creating ZIP archives locally. This library runs within the extension sandbox and does not transmit data.

## Data Retention
All image data and settings are stored locally in your {{BROWSER_NAME}}. You can clear this data at any time by:
1. Opening {{STORE_URL}}
2. Finding "{{EXTENSION_NAME}}"
3. Clicking "Remove" or clearing extension data

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the \"Last Updated\" date.
